# Example prompts

- Check my MusVideo credit balance and list my recent music videos.
- Turn this song into a 9:16 music video with a fictional performer and a dreamy city setting: [my public audio URL].
- Show me the story for project [project ID] before we approve it.
- I approve the displayed story. Get the render quote, but wait for my approval of the cost.
- How is project [project ID] progressing? If finished, show the video.
- Cancel the active render for project [project ID]. Tell me what has already been charged.

Cost approval examples are separate turns. These prompts do not grant standing permission for arbitrary future spending. Never insert someone else's project ID or signed media URL as a public example.
